# Scraping Service Market Research
*Date: 2026-02-16*

## 1. Fiverr Web Scraping Services

**What top sellers offer:**
- Custom web scrapers (Python/Selenium/Playwright)
- Data extraction from specific sites (Google Maps, LinkedIn, Amazon, Yelp)
- Lead list generation (emails, phones, names)
- Price monitoring / competitor research bots

**Pricing:**
- Basic gigs: $5–$50 (low quality, often just CSV dumps)
- Mid-tier: $50–$200 (custom scraper for specific site)
- Pro/top-rated: $200–$1,000+ (complex multi-site, anti-bot bypass)
- Ongoing scraping maintenance: $100–$500/mo

**Assessment:** OVERSATURATED. Race to the bottom on price. Differentiation = reliability, speed, and niche expertise.

---

## 2. Government Data Scraping

**Key challenges:**
- .gov sites often block datacenter IPs (403 errors)
- Proxy services restrict .gov access or require KYC + $500+ minimum
- Many govt sites use legacy tech, PDFs, CAPTCHAs
- GSA guidance: public govt data is generally scrapeable if respecting robots.txt

**Hardest sites:** SAM.gov (rate limits), state court systems (PACER), municipal permit portals (fragmented across 10K+ jurisdictions)

**Who's doing it:** ScrapeHero, xByte, custom consultancies. Very few Fiverr sellers.

**Assessment:** UNDERSERVED. High barrier to entry = less competition. Huge demand from govt contractors, real estate investors, journalists.

## 3. B2B Lead Generation Data Providers

**Market pricing:**
- Fiverr lead lists: $5–$50 (garbage quality)
- Credit-based providers (Apollo, ZoomInfo, Lusha): $500–$3,500/mo for datasets
- Full-service B2B lead gen agencies: $2,500–$12,000/mo
- Budget tools (Adapt.io, SalesHandy): $29–$100/mo for 1,000 credits
- ListKit, LeadsForge: mid-market, pay-per-lead model

**What small businesses actually pay:** $50–$500 for a one-time niche list. $100–$300/mo for ongoing leads.

**Assessment:** HUGE market but dominated by big players (ZoomInfo, Apollo). Opportunity in NICHE lists (specific industries, geographies) that big players don't curate well.

---

## 4. SAM.gov Federal Contracts Data

**Scrapeable?** YES — SAM.gov has a public API (api.sam.gov) with free API keys. Opportunities API returns JSON. Apify has a SAM.gov scraper actor.

**What's available:** Contract opportunities, solicitations, awards, entity registrations, NAICS codes, set-aside types, agencies.

**Demand:** High among govt contractors, GovCon consultants, BD teams. $2.7T market. Companies like GovConAPI.com exist just to provide easier access to this data.

**Assessment:** STRONG OPPORTUNITY. The API exists but is clunky. Value-add = curated alerts, filtered datasets, enriched data (add company size, past performance).

---

## 5. Google Places API / Business Data Enrichment

**Official API pricing:**
- $200/mo free credit from Google
- Place Details: ~2.5 cents per call (can add up fast)
- Nearby Search: $32 per 1,000 requests
- Limited to 60 results per query

**Alternatives:**
- Apify Google Maps scraper: bypasses 120 result limit, unlimited data
- Outscraper: free tier + pay-as-you-go Google Maps API
- Direct scraping: free but requires anti-bot measures

**What you get:** Business name, address, phone, website, hours, rating, review count, photos, popular times, price level.

**Assessment:** Core infrastructure for B2B list building. Cost is manageable if smart about caching. The data is gold for local business lists.

## 6. Dental Practice Contact Lists

**Market:**
- Hippo Direct, MedicoReach, LeadsPlease, SalesGenie: established list providers
- Pricing: typically $0.10–$0.50 per contact for basic info
- Full dental professional databases: 200K+ contacts available
- Custom-quoted (no public pricing) — usually $200–$2,000 depending on filters

**What buyers want:** Name, practice name, address, phone, email, specialty, NPI number, years in practice, practice size.

**Assessment:** PROVEN DEMAND. Dental supply companies, insurance, SaaS for dentists all buy these lists. Competition exists but data freshness is a differentiator.

## 7. VC Investor Contact Databases

**Market & pricing:**
- AngelMatch: 110K+ investors, ~$99–$299 one-time
- VC-Database.com: $197 one-time (7,500+ firms)
- VCPro Database: subscription model
- OpenVC: free but limited
- Massinvestor: 5,000+ investors
- Crunchbase Pro: $49/mo (industry standard)
- PitchBook: $20K+/year (enterprise)

**What people pay:** $100–$300 for a one-time list. $50–$100/mo for updated databases. Enterprise: $1K–$20K/year.

**Assessment:** HIGH DEMAND from founders raising capital. Market is served but data freshness and accuracy are constant pain points. Opportunity in niche VC lists (by sector, stage, geography).

## 8. Building Permits Data & Real Estate

**Demand:** Very high among:
- Real estate investors (identify development hotspots)
- Contractors (find upcoming projects, bid on work)
- Home service companies (target new construction/renovations)
- Insurance companies

**Who's doing it:** ScrapeHero, xByte, 3i Data Scraping — all offer real estate data packages including permits.

**Challenge:** Building permit data is fragmented across thousands of municipal websites. No central database.

**Assessment:** UNDERSERVED & HIGH VALUE. The fragmentation is the moat — hard to scale, so few do it well. Companies pay $500–$5,000+ for curated permit datasets by geography.

## Summary: Where to Focus

### 🟢 Best Opportunities (Underserved + High Demand)
1. **Government contracts (SAM.gov)** — API exists, demand is huge, few good middlemen
2. **Building permits by geography** — fragmented data = competitive moat

5. **Google Maps business data** — easy to scrape, lots of competition, but always in demand

7. **Generic web scraping on Fiverr** — race to the bottom
8. **LinkedIn scraping** — legal gray area, tons of tools already
